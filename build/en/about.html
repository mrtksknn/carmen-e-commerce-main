Piece Of Oblivion / About the Artist

Between what is looked at and what is felt.

Beliz Keskin

Beliz Keskin was born in Ankara in 1998. Keskin completed primary, middle and high school in Ankara. In 2020 Keskin graduated from the Department of Radio, Television and Cinema at Gazi University.

Placing the inner world of people, their suppressed emotions and the realities they avoid facing at the centre of the practice, Keskin holds that art should not be limited to offering an aesthetic experience.

Approach

Confrontation

In Keskin’s view, art should shake the viewer’s emotional comfort zone; it should call habitual patterns of thought into question and open a space for a person to face the emotions they avoid feeling.

Questioning

Keskin believes that a true artistic experience should create in the viewer not only admiration but also questioning and an inner conflict.

Distance

In the paintings, Keskin aims to make this approach visible by bringing the viewer face to face with what is disturbing, and to remove the distance between what is looked at and what is felt.
